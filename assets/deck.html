02 · The Product

Savings goals, quests, leaderboard
& soulbound SVG badges.

On-Chain Savings Loop

  • Create named goals: Target amount + 7, 30, or 90-day duration presets stored directly in BastionVault.
  • Deposit canonical MUSD: 1:1 transferable bMUSD receipt + live goal progress bar & confetti.
  • Levels & Quests: Cross 25% Bronze, 50% Silver, 75% Gold, and 100% Vanguard milestones plus 6 lifetime core quests.
  • Live Leaderboard: Ranked by on-chain balance, level, and badge count via getLeaderboard().

10 Soulbound ERC-721 Badges

  • 6 Core Milestones: First Deposit, Saved 100 MUSD, Saved 500 MUSD, Held 7 Days, Held 30 Days, Earned 10 MUSD Yield.
  • 4 Goal Tier Badges: Goal 25% Bronze, 50% Silver, 75% Gold, 100% Vanguard.
  • On-chain artwork: Full SVG generated inside BastionAchievements (tokenURI).
  • Zero off-chain backend: No database or centralized indexer required.

03 · Level 3+ Mezo Integration

Real yield from Mezo Stability Pool + Pools Router.

Every MUSD deposited leaves no idle treasury subsidies: Bastion supplies canonical MUSD directly into Mezo's Stability Pool and converts BTC liquidation gains back into MUSD.

Step 1

Canonical Deposit

User approves MUSD (0x1189...503) and calls depositToGoal().

Step 2

Stability Pool

Vault calls provideToSP(amount) on Mezo Stability Pool (0x1CCA...680e).

Step 3

BTC → MUSD Harvest

Permissionless harvest() claims BTC collateral gains and swaps via Mezo Pools Router (0x9a1f...7E9).

Step 4

Real Index Payout

90% raises accruedPerShare; 10% goes to treasury. UI displays realized on-chain APY only.

04 · Business Model & Sustainability

Protocol revenue tied strictly to real yield.

10% Yield Fee (On-Chain)

Treasury Performance Fee

Configured on-chain at protocolFeeBps = 1000 (capped at 25%). Every harvest splits realized MUSD automatically: 90% to bMUSD savers, 10% directly to the Bastion treasury. Zero principal fees.

Unit Economics

Self-Sustaining Savings Layer

  • Zero emissions burn: No inflationary reward token or treasury giveaway required.
  • Composable bMUSD: ERC-20 receipt token retains yield eligibility across transfers.
  • Future B2B quests: Protocol partners sponsor custom savings cohorts post-hackathon.
